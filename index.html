Toy Tumble

Our newest game we've created is called Toy Tumble, inspired by games like "Jump King" and "Getting Over It With Bennett Foddy". The game is supposed to be a "Foddian" game which is a genre of game known for their difficulty and lack of checkpoints.

You play as Chudley, a neglected toy who wants to escape to brighter pastures! Players are encouraged to compete for the fastest completion time! My fastest time to complete the level was 2 minutes!

Foul Fowls

We were very inspired by the swinging mechanics of the Spider-Man games for this project, so we created Foul Fowls!

In this game you play as Earl. E Byrd, a rejected super hero in charge of saving the town of Birbingham from the nefarious Robin Banks' evil pigeons.

Super Hamster Quest

This retro inspired game takes notes from classic top down Legend of Zelda, the game is a short challenging dungeon the player has to fight through in order to reach the boss at the end.

The main characters in this game are Hamus and Hamson, two spoiled hamsters who've had their home invaded by rats! They must stop the rats from stealing all their tasty food.

Bun Bounce

Bounce around and search for carrots in this cute platformer! This was one of my first projects and I think it turned out pretty good, it is great to have a visual reference to see how far I have come in terms of game development.

Help Bun find all of their carrots, the game may be simple to control but the levels can prove quite the challenge!
